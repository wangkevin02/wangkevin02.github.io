Kuang Wang

Ph.D. Student in Computer Science · CUHK-Shenzhen · advised by Prof. Haizhou Li

Speech · Language · Human-centered AI

I am a Ph.D. student at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen. I study how speech and language models can better understand what a person means, how an utterance should sound, and who the system is interacting with.

My current work centers on expressive speech generation and Speech Language Models (SLMs), with broader interests in personalized language models, user simulation, and conversational AI. Before CUHK-Shenzhen, I received my B.Eng. in Biosystems Engineering from Zhejiang University.

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the [School of Data Science](https://sds.cuhk.edu.cn/en),
[The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen](https://www.cuhk.edu.cn/en), advised by
[Prof. Haizhou Li](https://sds.cuhk.edu.cn/en/teacher/498). Before that, I received my B.Eng.
in Biosystems Engineering from [Zhejiang University](https://www.zju.edu.cn/english/).

My research focuses on building **more natural and adaptive human–AI interactions** through
speech and language. I am particularly interested in how conversational systems can better
understand their users and communicate in more expressive and human-like ways. My current
work includes **user modeling, expressive speech generation, and Speech Language Models (SLMs)**.

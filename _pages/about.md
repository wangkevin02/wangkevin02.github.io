I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the [School of Data Science](https://sds.cuhk.edu.cn/en),
[The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen](https://www.cuhk.edu.cn/en), advised by
[Prof. Haizhou Li](https://sds.cuhk.edu.cn/en/teacher/498). Before that, I received my B.Eng.
in Biosystems Engineering from [Zhejiang University](https://www.zju.edu.cn/english/).

My research focuses on building **more natural, real-time, and adaptive human–AI interactions**
through speech, vision, and language. I am particularly interested in how conversational systems can
understand users deeply, listen and speak seamlessly in full-duplex settings, and communicate with
human-like expressiveness. My current work spans **Speech Language Models (SLMs), full-duplex
multimodal dialogue, expressive speech generation, and user modeling**.
